# Step 3: sequence QC and feature definitions

Generated by `ouput/analysis/features_step3.py` from `data/Information_for_Baris_corrected.xlsx`
(sheet `Information for feeding`). Outputs in `ouput/analysis/step3/`.

Every quantity below is a **descriptor computed from the supplied sequences**. None has been
validated against secretion, and none is evidence of mechanism. Features are computed for all
32 peptides — strong, intermediate and weak performers alike — before any outcome is consulted,
so that feature selection cannot be driven by the outcome.

### QC finding to confirm with the data owner: SP31

`SP31` is the single discrepancy, and it is a one-character disagreement at residue 1:

- listed amino-acid sequence: `VRKSLITLGLASVIGTSSFLIPFTSKTASA` (begins with **V**)
- its nucleotide sequence begins `ATG…`, which translates to **M**
- residues 2–30 agree perfectly

Every other peptide begins with M, and `ATG` is the initiator codon, so the listed `V` is most
likely a typographical error for `M`. **This has not been corrected.** The feature table uses the
amino-acid sequence exactly as supplied and carries a `qc_flag` on this row. The effect of the
choice is confined to one residue of one peptide, but it should be resolved by the owner rather
than assumed, in the same way `S19`, `Np` and the Greek `Η` were resolved in meeting 2.

### Library design constant: the cleavage motif does not vary

All 32 peptides end in **A-S-A**. Positions −3, −2 and −1 are therefore identical across the
entire library, and the strict A-x-A cleavage motif is present in 32 of 32 peptides.

This matters for interpretation. Fu et al. (2018) highlight an A-x-A-like C-region motif among
their top performers, but in this library that motif is a **design constant**, not a variable.
A constant cannot explain any difference in secretion between these peptides. `axa_like_motif`
is therefore excluded from association testing (recorded in `constant_features.csv`), and the
search for explanatory variation is restricted to the n-region, the hydrophobic core, overall
length/composition, and nucleotide-level properties. Position −4 does vary and is retained as
a categorical descriptor.

## 2. Region segmentation, and why two versions exist

No SignalP or trained signal-peptide predictor was used; none was available offline, and the
owner has supplied no experimental cleavage data. Regions are therefore derived by a transparent
rule that can be audited and varied.

**Variable-length h-region (`find_h_region`).** Maximum-subarray (Kadane) search for the
contiguous segment maximising total Kyte–Doolittle hydropathy in excess of a threshold
(primary setting: threshold 1.0, minimum length 5). The segment may not begin at residue 1, so
the initiator methionine always falls in the n-region, and may not reach the final residue, so a
c-region always exists. `n_region` is everything before it, `c_region` everything after.

**Stability check.** The same search was repeated under four settings (Kyte–Doolittle at
thresholds 0.5, 1.0 and 1.5, and the Eisenberg consensus scale at 0.0). Results in
`h_region_segmentation_sensitivity.csv` and `h_region_stability_summary.csv`:

| Quantity | Across-setting range (median) | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| h-region **start** | 0 residues (75th pct ≤ 0.5) | **stable** |
| h-region **length** | 4 residues (mean 5.0, max 18) | **unstable** |

Spearman correlation of h-region length between the three Kyte–Doolittle thresholds is only
0.30, 0.45 and −0.02. In other words, **where the hydrophobic stretch begins is reproducible,
but where it ends is not.** Any feature that depends on the h/c boundary — h-region length,
c-region length, and composition fractions computed over those spans — inherits that
instability.

**Robust companion (`hydrophobic_core`).** To avoid resting conclusions on an unstable
boundary, a fixed-width 9-residue window of maximal mean Kyte–Doolittle hydropathy is also
computed. Its width is fixed, so the only free quantity is its position, which the sensitivity
analysis shows is stable. Derived from it:

- `n_prox_*` — the residues *before* the core (stable boundary)
- `h_core9_*` — composition and hydropathy *of* the core
- `c_prox6_*` — the fixed last 6 residues, which depend on no boundary choice at all

`feature_stability_classification.csv` labels every descriptor `robust` or
`fragile_depends_on_h_c_boundary`. **Step 4 tests the robust set; the fragile set is reported
only as sensitivity.**

## 3. Feature list

### Whole-peptide physicochemical (robust)

| Feature | Definition / method |
|---|---|
| `aa_length` | residue count of the listed sequence |
| `gravy_kd` | mean Kyte–Doolittle hydropathy (Biopython `ProteinAnalysis.gravy`) |
| `aromaticity` | Lobry–Gautier aromaticity (F+W+Y fraction), Biopython |
| `instability_index` | Guruprasad instability index, Biopython — *exploratory; designed for whole globular proteins, not 30-residue peptides* |
| `isoelectric_point` | Biopython `IsoelectricPoint.pi()` |
| `net_charge_ph7` | Biopython `charge_at_pH(7.0)` |
| `kr_count`, `de_count`, `kr_minus_de` | simple residue counts |
| `frac_hydrophobic_AVILMFWY`, `frac_leucine`, `frac_alanine`, `frac_glycine`, `frac_proline`, `frac_serine`, `frac_helix_breaker_GPS` | composition fractions over the full sequence |

### Region-based (robust)

| Feature | Definition |
|---|---|
| `h_core9_start_1based` | start position of the maximal-hydropathy 9-residue window |
| `h_core9_mean_kd` | mean Kyte–Doolittle hydropathy of that window |
| `h_core9_frac_leucine`, `h_core9_frac_hydrophobic`, `h_core9_frac_helix_breaker_GPS` | composition of that window |
| `n_prox_length` | residues before the core (= `h_core9_start_1based` − 1) |
| `n_prox_kr_count`, `n_prox_net_charge_ph7`, `n_prox_frac_basic_KR` | n-region positive-charge descriptors — the feature Fu et al. associate with lysine-rich N-regions |
| `c_prox6_gravy`, `c_prox6_frac_polar_STNQ`, `c_prox6_frac_small_AGS` | composition of the fixed last 6 residues |
| `pos_minus4` | residue at position −4 (categorical; −3/−2/−1 are constant) |

### Nucleotide and transcript (robust, with an important caveat)

| Feature | Definition |
|---|---|
| `nt_gc_fraction` | GC fraction of the signal-peptide coding sequence |
| `nt_gc3_fraction` | GC fraction at third codon positions |
| `nt_gc_first30` | GC fraction of the first 30 bases |
| `mrna_dg_first30nt`, `mrna_dg_first48nt`, `mrna_dg_first60nt` | ViennaRNA 2.7.2 `RNA.fold` minimum free energy (kcal/mol) of the first 30/48/60 bases, T→U |
| `mrna_dg_full_sp_cds`, `mrna_dg_full_per_nt` | MFE of the whole signal-peptide CDS, raw and per nucleotide |

**Caveat on the folding energies.** Fu et al. (2018) computed folding free energy for the region
*around the translation initiation site*, which includes the 5′UTR, the Shine–Dalgarno/ribosome
binding site and vector context. The owner offered to supply the RBS, start, stop and terminator
context in meeting 2, but **those records have not been received.** The windows computed here
begin at the start codon of the signal peptide and contain no upstream sequence. They are
therefore *not* the same quantity Fu et al. reported, and cannot be used to confirm or refute
that paper's observation. They are included as transparent coding-sequence descriptors only.
Because the upstream context is reported to be identical across constructs, differences between
peptides in a true initiation-region calculation would still be driven by the coding sequence —
but that is an expectation, not a verified equivalence, and the absolute values would differ.

### Not computed

- **SignalP D-score** — no offline SignalP installation; not computed rather than approximated.
- **Codon adaptation index** — a reference set of highly expressed *B. subtilis* genes would be
  required. A CAI computed against this library's own 32 sequences would be circular and is not
  reported.
- **Predicted 3-D structure / AlphaFold features** — see `analysis_plan.md` §6b. Not computed,
  and would in any case be exploratory rather than mechanistic.

## 4. Feature redundancy

`feature_collinearity_spearman.csv` and `feature_collinearity_heatmap.png` report all
pairwise Spearman correlations. 22 pairs reach |ρ| ≥ 0.8 and are listed in
`feature_redundant_pairs.csv`.

These descriptors are **not independent tests.** Length, hydrophobic fraction, GRAVY and
core hydropathy all measure overlapping properties of the same short sequence, as do pI,
net charge and K/R count. This has two consequences carried into step 4: a naive count of
"how many features correlate with secretion" overstates the evidence, and a per-feature
p-value treated as independent is misleading. Associations are therefore reported with
explicit multiplicity caveats and judged primarily on consistency of direction across the
three independently normalised blots, not on nominal significance.
