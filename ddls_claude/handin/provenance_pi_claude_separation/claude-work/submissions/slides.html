Do signal-peptide features predict
collagen secretion in B. subtilis?

32 peptides · three Western blots · one collagen construct

Baris Akar · bakar@dtu.dk · 4 November 2026
Data owner: Alexiou et al., thesis chapter 2

The question

Three questions from the data owner

  • Do sequence, nucleotide or physicochemical features relate to secretion?
  • If so, can they identify or design high-secretion candidates?
  • Is it worth training a model?

The owner stated that a well-supported negative result would be informative, and cautioned against analysing only the best performers — the exact flaw they had seen in the published literature.

The data

Effective n is 8, 13 and 11 — not 32

Blot 1 Blot 2 Blot 3 peptide mean

Each blot was divided by the mean of a different peptide subset, so the three panels share no numeric scale. The dashed line is each gel's own mean.

Method

Four rules — three of them the owner's own cautions

  • Never pool the blots. Combine per-blot effects by meta-analysis, never the values.
  • One value per peptide. 96 replicate rows → 32 observations before any test.
  • All 32 candidates, never only the top performers.
  • Split at peptide level, scaling fitted inside training folds only.

34 descriptors computed before any outcome was consulted, so feature choice could not be steered by results. Region boundaries were tested for stability under four settings; boundary-dependent features were demoted to sensitivity only.

Result 3

In-sample fit is not performance

In-sample — fitted and scored on the same peptides Out-of-fold — predicting held-out peptides

34 features, 32 observations → the model memorises the set and learns nothing transferable. Every out-of-fold score is negative under both validation schemes, including leave-one-blot-out — the realistic test of a new experiment.

Why it could not work

The library suppresses its own variation

Native C-termini span 13 motifs. All 32 supplied peptides end A-S-A. Only 8 of 32 match their native sequence exactly.

The cleavage region was engineered, not inherited — so the A-x-A motif Fu et al. link to good performers is a constant here, not a variable.

Hydrophobicity is range-restricted too: GRAVY median 1.01, with 26 of 32 between 0.6 and 1.4 — sitting on the optimum published by Grasso et al. SP1/AmyE, the classic secretion tag, ranks 7th of 8 and is among the most modified peptides.

Why it could not work

Four reasons, in order

  • No signal to learn. Best feature 0.369; shuffled data gives 0.441. Nothing for a model to combine.
  • Effective n is 8 / 13 / 11 against 34 features → memorisation, not learning.
  • Not a noise problem. Reliability ceiling 0.86 — there was room to see an effect.
  • Design holds key features fixed. Constant cleavage region, constant mature-protein start, restricted hydrophobicity range.

A negative result about this dataset's shape — not about the biology, and not a criticism of the experiment.

The one honest positive

mRNA accessibility at the initiation region

Positive in all three blots, in the direction Fu et al. predicted. Combined ρ +0.515; one-sided permutation p = 0.0042 (0.0118 corrected for three window sizes). Computable only because the thesis supplied the shared RBS.

Caveat: a coding-only version of this family was already in the 34-feature screen — a refinement, not clean out-of-sample confirmation. True transcription start site unknown. No mechanism established.

Recommendation

What the owner can and cannot act on

Can

Prioritise SP5 (CV 0.14) and SP26 (CV 0.21) — high and reproducible within their own blots.

Treat SP4 with caution — 1st by mean, 5th by median, plus the unresolved secondary band.

Test the mRNA-accessibility hypothesis.

Cannot

Build a cross-blot ranking.

Train a predictive model or propose synthetic designs.

Claim a mechanism.

Assume transfer to another protein target — five peptides shared with Fu et al. give ρ = −0.20.

Next steps

What would make the question answerable

  • A shared reference lane on every gel. The single largest fix — it turns three incomparable blots into one dataset. Nothing analytical substitutes for it.
  • More peptides on that common scale. Grasso et al. needed ~4,400.
  • Raw densitometry — audit normalisation, resolve the SP9 zero, assess SP4's secondary band.
  • Transcription start site and 5′UTR — to test the lead properly.
  • Confirm two anomalies: SP31's V vs M, and whether the uniform A-S-A was intentional.
