# Analysis outputs (descriptive steps 2–8, documented features, exploratory modeling)

Descriptive outputs generated by `run_steps_2_8.py`; comprehensive Step 3 features by `features_step3.py`; Step 4 associations by `associations_step4.py`; supplemental sequence features by `build_documented_features.py`; exploratory model metrics by `exploratory_peptide_models.py`. Source: `data/Information_for_Baris_corrected.xlsx`.

- `replicate_summary_by_peptide.csv`: per peptide/block mean, median, sample SD, min/max, CV, within-block mean rank.
- `replicate_values_long.csv`: source cell and numeric replicate-level values.
- `replicate_rank_concordance.csv`: pairwise replicate Spearman/Kendall rank consistency within each block.
- `replicate_values_by_blot.png`: replicate values faceted by blot; each has its own axis/scale.
- `validation_paired_shared_peptides.csv`, `validation_comparison_stats.csv`, `validation_shared_peptides.png`: descriptive paired comparison for seven shared candidates. No calibration was fitted.
- `sequence_qc_and_features.csv`: sequence alphabet/length/translation QC and transparent full-listed-sequence features. No cleavage boundaries are assumed.
- `sequence_descriptors_exploratory.csv`: additional composition/position summaries (N-terminal K/R, a heuristic internal region, and C-terminal motif pattern); positional boundaries are conventions, not verified biological boundaries.
- `documented_sequence_features.csv`, `documented_feature_dictionary.csv`, `documented_sequence_features_README.md`, `build_documented_features.py`: reproducible AA region/physicochemical, nucleotide composition and ViennaRNA CDS-window MFE features, with definitions, settings, sequence QC, and limitations. SP31's listed AA/nt translation mismatch is explicitly flagged; source sequences are not silently corrected.
- `exploratory_peptide_models.py`, `exploratory_model_metrics_by_blot.csv`, `exploratory_model_loo_predictions.csv`, `exploratory_peptide_models_README.md`: per-original-blot LOO Ridge regression compared with a training-mean baseline; one outcome per peptide, no cross-blot pooling, explicitly exploratory.
- `within_blot_feature_associations.csv`, `within_blot_feature_associations.png`: descriptive associations within each original blot; no combined cross-blot outcome is used. P-values are exploratory and unadjusted.
- `other_protein_categories_long.csv`, `other_protein_categories_matrix.csv`, `other_protein_category_counts.csv`, `other_protein_categories_heatmap.png`: corrected categorical labels kept categorical.

Meeting 2 added owner-reported clarifications: replicates are described as biological (exact unit/independence unclear); values are ordered left-to-right by ascending replicate mean for presentation; normalization is described as lane-total-protein normalization followed by division by the gel-wide mean of normalized band intensities; and category `No` means product not produced. The owner interprets SP9's recorded zero as likely no secretion but is unsure of its origin. These statements have not been audited against raw assay records; `No` remains categorical and the zero remains numeric. The first-nine-nucleotide GC proxy is exploratory only because translation-initiation sequence boundaries are unknown. No supervised ML model or AlphaFold model was generated; the reasons and limitations are documented in `ouput/md/analysis_plan.md`.
